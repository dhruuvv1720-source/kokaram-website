OUR STORY

From 1950 to Today

Kokaram & Sons began its journey in 1950 and has grown with the people of Mathura. Through changing times, one thing has remained constant — the trust of our customers and our focus on dependable quality.

From premium dry fruits to our well-known hing and carefully selected herbs, we continue to serve the everyday needs of homes, kitchens and customers looking for authentic ingredients.
